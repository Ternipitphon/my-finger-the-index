CapriCore v1.2.2 + Caprii integration (Windows x64)

Based on CapriChooms/CapriCore v1.2.2, commit 828f27243df3d1b9669cbe1a5d16633b68c1108a.
Original character, artwork, model/voice engines, native Spotify, desktop controls and permissions are retained.
Adds a persisted Short / Balanced / Detailed reply preference in Conversation settings, including Thai labels, preview and discard support.
Uses this fork's GitHub updates; the upstream mirror cannot overwrite the integration.

Download CapriSetup-1.2.2.exe to install. This is a separate integration preview, not an official CapriChooms release. The installer is unsigned. Optional AI models and voices are downloaded during setup; Thai local speech synthesis is not added in this build.
Source and checksums are included. Project code remains GPL-3.0-only. Original copyright, LICENSE and third-party notices are retained; character, voice and third-party asset rights are separate.
Automated checks do not verify your Spotify account, speakers, microphone or GPU. Keep your existing installation and private data until you have tried this build.
