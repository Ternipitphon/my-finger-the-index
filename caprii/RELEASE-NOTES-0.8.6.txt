Caprii 0.8.6 — distinct Playful and Caring personalities

Right-click Caprii → Personality / นิสัย → Playful / กวน ๆ, Caring / เป็นห่วง, or Normal / ปกติ. Playful remains the default and existing Playful/Normal selections migrate unchanged. Caring is a separate opt-in style, saved across restarts.

Playful emphasizes brief, friendly situational teasing grounded in visible facts, without routinely turning games or many browser tabs into unsolicited concern/break reminders. Caring emphasizes warmth and considerate check-ins without diagnosing fatigue or distress from a screenshot alone. All modes remain Caprii, keep factual answers, preserve exact user/tool values and JSON, and respect permissions. Genuine necessary risk guidance is retained. Old bond/mood/jealousy instructions no longer override an explicitly selected style in chat or screen observations.

The selection applies to chat, screen observations and attached-image replies. It does not enable microphone or automatic screen watching and does not change character artwork, voice choice or action routing. Generated phrasing still varies by model.

Test Lab 1.14.0 retains the eight DOM cases and separately records the personality review contract. Windows x64 installer is unsigned. Close Caprii from the tray before installing; select Caring only if desired. Source, build information, checksums and original license notices are attached.
